## 1. Demo Flow Pivot (D9)

Mei and I agreed that starting the demo with the full atlas graph loses people immediately. Judges can’t parse "why you two connect" if they don’t know who the people are yet.

**New flow:**
1. **Open on ONE specific connection.** Pick a pair of users with a strong, explainable link (e.g., "rare direct match on distributed systems + shared interest in Raft"). Show the "Why You Connect" card first. Let the judge read the explanation.
2. **Zoom out to the Atlas.** Once they understand *why* those two connect, pull back to show the turquoise atlas — interests as soft overlapping regions, people as spheres inside them. This is where we show scale and the "map" metaphor.
3. **Show the pipeline (briefly).** One slide or quick verbal nod: "This works because we’re resolving free-text interests to canonical concepts locally." Don’t dive into trigrams/embeddings unless asked.
4. **Mention privacy.** Diego’s been stressing this. We need to explicitly say: "All model inference happens on local hardware; no data leaves the box."

**Action:** Jane needs to build a "demo mode" toggle that forces the app to open on the pre-selected user pair, not the generic home screen. Must be ready by **Fri 11 Sep EOD**.
