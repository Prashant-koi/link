## Goals
We have ~10 weeks until the HackMIT / Dropbox hackathon (Sat 12 – Sun 13 Sep). The pitch is simple: **Link** surfaces connections between university members by mapping what people know and want to learn onto a shared concept graph.

- **Core value:** People who share rare concepts are ranked higher than those who share common ones.
- **The "Why":** Every suggestion must explain *why* two people connect (shared interests, related fields). This is our differentiator.
- **Privacy:** All AI inference runs locally on hardware we control. No personal interest data leaves the machine.

## Key Technical Decisions (Initial)
1.  **Datastore:** Postgres 16 with `pgvector`. We are *not* using Neo4j. One datastore is simpler to maintain, and recursive CTEs handle our graph needs fine for now. *(Chen argued this; Jane accepted after seeing the query complexity was manageable.)*
2.  **Model Strategy:** Local inference only. We will start with `qwen2.5-14b` via Ollama on John’s ASUS GX10 (128GB RAM). If latency is too high, we’ll look at smaller quantized models or batching.
3.  **Resolution Pipeline:**
    -   Exact alias match
    -   Trigram fuzzy match (threshold TBD)
    -   Embedding nearest-neighbor (`nomic-embed-text`)
    -   LLM adjudication (only if confidence is low)
    -   *Goal:* Minimize LLM calls to keep latency down and privacy tight.
