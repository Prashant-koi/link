## Scope of what we're claiming
We are **not** claiming novelty in entity linking or ontology matching per se. Our contribution is the *pipeline composition* and the *local-model adjudication step* that keeps personal interest strings off cloud APIs. Everything below is grouped by the stage of our pipeline it touches.

## Entity / concept linking
- Classical entity linking (EL) assumes a fixed knowledge base and a mention-to-entity task; closest to our "exact alias" and "trigram fuzzy" stages. [cite: EL survey, e.g. LREC/ACL position paper]
- Fuzzy string matching (trigrams, `pg_trgm`) is standard for alias expansion in schema-on-read systems. We use a 0.6 threshold; note this is *tuned*, not derived from literature — flag as an ablation candidate. [cite: trigram similarity / pg_trgm docs]
- Our alias cache means each free-text string hits the model at most once; this is closer to a learned synonym table than to online EL. TODO: compare against [cite: dictionary-based normalisation in NLP4EDU].

## Ontology matching & alignment
- Ontology matching literature (AOL, OAEI) typically aligns two schemas; we are doing *mention-to-concept* against one fixed CS subset of the ontology. Different task, but the "candidate generation + disambiguation" split is shared. [cite: ontology matching survey]
- Hierarchy-aware scoring: we add a decayed 1-hop bonus (currently 0.15). Related to "semantic distance" penalties in matching scores — need one or two refs before Sofia sees this. [cite: semantic similarity in ontology alignment]

## LLM-based normalisation / adjudication
- Recent work on using LLMs for data cleaning and entity resolution is mostly cloud-API based (GPT-4, etc.). Our distinction: the adjudicator is a 27B local instruct model with reasoning disabled, chosen for reproducibility and privacy. [cite: LLM-for-data-cleaning papers, e.g. 2024/2025]
- "Thinking" models (reasoning-enabled) vs. standard instruct: we explicitly run `reasoning_effort=none`. TODO: find one paper that benchmarks reasoning-on vs. off for classification-style tasks; if none exists, state it as a practical finding, not a citation. [cite: TBD]
- Small-model substitution: our 4B instruct model agreed with the 27B on 37/40 adjudication cases but failed 2/10 junk strings. Related to "distillation for constrained deployment" — weak fit; keep as one sentence. [cite: model distillation survey]

## Privacy-preserving / local LLM deployment
- On-device and on-prem LLM inference is increasingly common for PII-heavy tasks. Our motivation (Diego's privacy constraint, D3) aligns with this line. [cite: local-LLM-for-privacy papers]
- Ollama-style keep-warm + batching to avoid cold-start reload latency — we hit a 46 s reload penalty; our fix (2-min heartbeat, batch=256, concurrency=2) is an engineering detail but worth one paragraph since it's the reason the system is *usable* locally. [cite: Ollama docs / inference-serving papers]

## What is NOT covered (so we don't overclaim)
- We do not train or fine-tune any model; all weights are off-the-shelf.
- We do not propose a new ontology; the CS subset is fixed input.
- User-side "why you connect" explanations are generated by the same local model but are out of scope for this paper (mention only).
